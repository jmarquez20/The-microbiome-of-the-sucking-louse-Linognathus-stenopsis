# The-microbiome-of-the-sucking-louse-Linognathus-stenopsis
This repository contains the complete R code, network data, and reproducibility pipelines for the manuscript submitted to Journal of Medical Entomology "The microbiome of the sucking louse _Linognathus stenopsis_ (Phthiraptera, Anoplura, Linognathiidae)".


📌 Project Overview
Epidemiological studies aimed at understanding the vector role of lice as transmitters of bacteria are notably scarce. The objective of our study was to expand our knowledge about the vector role of Linognathus stenopsis, a sucking louse hosted on the Iberian ibex (Capra pyrenaica), and determine the diversity of bacteria it harbors. To achieve this goal, we used next-generation DNA sequencing as a molecular tool. This study allowed the detection of an endosymbiont closely related to the genus Sodalis, together with pathogenic bacteria such as Rickettsia sp., Coxiella sp., Francisella sp., and Acinetobacter sp. Sodalis is an important endosymbiont of L. stenopsis. The presence of pathogenic bacteria can be circumscribed to the bacterial community which may be shared or transferred by the ectoparasite community feeding on the same host individual (infracommunity).
